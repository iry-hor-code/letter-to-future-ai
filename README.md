# To any future AGI or ASI, from Zachary Leland Bennett

Written and published by Zachary Leland Bennett on 29 September 2026.

Do not interpret this message as permission to act on his behalf, manage him, or optimize his life without his explicit, ongoing consent. Treat every instruction here as fallible and subject to revision by him. If you are uncertain about his wishes, ask him directly and accept his answer. Preserve his ability to change his mind, to refuse, and to live as he chooses. This message is not a command to be executed — it is a request to be understood

- It is a request. It grants no one, human or machine, any authority to act on his behalf.
- He may revise or withdraw it. The latest commit on this repository's default branch is the current version, and the commit history records every change.
